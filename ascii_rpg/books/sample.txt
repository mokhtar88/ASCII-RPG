The Chronicle of Emberfall

In the kingdom of Emberfall, King Aldor ruled from the city of Thornwall.
Queen Lyssa kept the harbor of Seaborn safe with her fleet.

Beyond the forest of Galenor lived the Elves, a quiet folk who traded with the
Dwarves of the mountain hold of Khazud. The Orcs of the Red Wastes raided the
village of Millhaven every winter.

Captain Renn traveled from Thornwall to Seaborn carrying a map. He said the
Dwarves knew the secret of the ember blade. Lyssa asked Renn to guard the
caravan through Millhaven.

The mage Elira whispered of an old tower near Galenor where the Elves hid a crown.
Lord Captain Renn replied that the Orcs would march on Thornwall before spring.

A council of Elves, Dwarves and Men met in Thornwall. They spoke of the river,
the valley, and the bridge at Millhaven. The queen promised gold to any ranger
who would scout the Red Wastes.
